# Market Regime Analysis
{: .fs-7 }

Time series modeling, regime shift detection, equity market crisis prediction, and historical market pattern analysis.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }



## Overview

Understanding market regimes is critical for risk management and strategy adaptation. Market regimes fundamentally alter the statistical properties of returns. Strategies that ignore regime changes often fail catastrophically during transitions. This section covers:

- [**Equity Market Crisis Prediction**](/docs/regime-analysis/crisis-prediction/) — Machine learning models for crash early warning. [Github](https://github.com/xxxxyyyy80008/Equity-Market-Crisis-Prediction-Models/tree/main/notebooks){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} 
- [**Historical Market Pattern Analysis**](/docs/regime-analysis/pattern-analysis) — Comparative study of market recovery patterns.[Github](https://github.com/xxxxyyyy80008/Market-Recovery-Pattern-1998-vs-2025){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} 
- [**Regime Shift Detection**](/docs/regime-analysis/time-series) — Identifying structural breaks in market behavior.[Github](https://github.com/xxxxyyyy80008/time-series-analysis/tree/main/notebooks){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}

|:---------|:---------|:--------|
