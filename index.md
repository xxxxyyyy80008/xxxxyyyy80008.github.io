## Research Areas

<div class="code-example" markdown="1">

### [Systematic Strategy Design](/docs/alpha-research/)
Robust signal engineering using Python. Focuses on **Walk-Forward Validation** to eliminate overfitting, utilizing strategies like Adaptive Moving Averages (AMA) and Volatility Breakouts (MABW).

### [Derivatives & Risk Analytics](/docs/derivatives/)
Stochastic calculus implementations for pricing options, computing real-time Greeks, and modeling Value-at-Risk (VaR/CVaR) via historical and Monte Carlo methods.

### [Portfolio Construction](/docs/portfolio-optimization/)
Advanced allocation frameworks including Mean-Variance, Risk Parity, and Hierarchical Clustering to maximize Sharpe ratios and minimize tail risk.

### [Market Regime Analysis](/docs/regime-analysis/)
Time series forecasting and regime shift detection using Hidden Markov Models (HMM) and Supervised Learning to adapt strategies to changing market volatility.

### [Competitions](/docs/competitions/)
Solutions for Kaggle quantitative finance challenges, including the Optiver Realized Volatility Prediction and Hull Tactical Asset Allocation.

</div>
